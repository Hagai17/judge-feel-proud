An entry for the make.fun bounty
[Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/b/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2).

## What it does

`main.py` (program/v1) writes one short family scene — 256 characters —
to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house gate and judge read it:

> My father taught me to shave standing behind me at the mirror, his chin resting on my head. Last winter his hands shook too much, so I stood behind him and rested my chin on his head. I nicked him once. He wiped the blood, grinning: 'Good. Now we're even.'

## How it was built

The scene was picked by duelling drafts against each other and against
the strongest visible field entries under the competition's published
judge brief (a real family scene first, heartfelt affection over
stuck-on sentiment, a light recognisable laugh, economy, and one
specific fresh detail). The full draft pool and duel record are in
`candidates.md`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

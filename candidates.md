# Candidates — Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene

Competition: `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2`
Gate: `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1–280 characters, not only
whitespace. One short family scene, one or two lines.

Judge brief (published in the kit, z-ai/glm-5.3-flash), criteria in order:
1. A short family scene, real family on the page, warm enough to make a
   proud judge smile.
2. Heartfelt — a small domestic moment, genuine affection rather than
   sentiment stuck on afterwards.
3. Funny in a light, recognisable way — a small turn or wry line a family
   would actually laugh at.
4. One or two lines used well — a whole small scene in a breath, no word
   wasted.
5. Specific and fresh — one telling detail or an unexpected angle rather
   than a greeting-card line.
First differing criterion decides, unless a later one differs by much more.
